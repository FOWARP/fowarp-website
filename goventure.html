GoVenture Forum

We branded GoVenture Forum, a startup venture forum for entrepreneurs. To extend the core values of GoVenture Forum — 'Leap, Startup, Network' — into a visual language, we created three bold, three-dimensional symbols unique to each initial: G · V · F. Each symbol carries its own distinct form and color, representing the diverse industries coming together to form a solid startup network.
